## Execution rule

For each candidate, keep the source research record as the owner of the optional handoff data. Add one route-local, plain-language link after the evidence content, refresh only that record's modification date, and prove the generated source H1, canonical URL, Article dates, exact link inside `<main>`, destination H1/canonical, and both sitemap records. The service guide is a planning path, not a promise that a Filipino caller can make approval, commercial, privacy, or customer-outcome decisions.
